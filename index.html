About the Editors

Endless Architectural Ideas is an independent journal edited by practicing architects and interior designers. We publish in-depth, ad-free articles for professionals and enthusiasts.

Frequently Asked

Common questions about our editorial approach and design philosophy.

Do you accept sponsored content?+
No. Endless Architectural Ideas is entirely reader-supported. We do not publish sponsored articles or affiliate links. Our recommendations are based on editorial merit.
Can I republish your articles?+
We welcome requests for syndication. Please contact our editorial team for permission and guidelines. Short excerpts with a link back are permitted under fair use.
How do you select the projects you feature?+
We look for projects that demonstrate thoughtful material use, spatial innovation, and a clear response to context—whether social, environmental, or cultural. We are not a portfolio; we tell stories.
Do you offer design consultations?+
We are a publication, not a design firm. However, our editors occasionally speak at events and teach workshops. Subscribe to our newsletter for updates.
