About the Journal

Independent editorial for architects, designers, and curious minds.

We believe good design is a form of quiet activism. Every material choice, every window, every threshold is a chance to improve daily life.

Endless Architectural Ideas began in 2018 as a personal notebook—a place to collect the details that make spaces feel alive. That notebook grew into a journal read by architects, interior designers, students, and enthusiasts across the world.

We are not a portfolio. We are not a news aggregator. We are a small, independent editorial team that publishes long-form articles about the built environment: residential architecture, interiors, materials, lighting, and spatial design.

Our Editorial Approach

Every article starts with a question: Why does this space feel the way it does? We look beyond surface aesthetics to understand the decisions—material, structural, spatial—that shape how we live.

We do not accept sponsored content. We do not use affiliate links. Our recommendations are based on editorial merit alone. When we feature a project, it is because it teaches us something worth sharing.

What We Cover

From rammed earth in Arizona to cross-laminated timber in Scandinavia, from layered lighting to compact living, we examine the ideas that move architecture forward—without losing sight of the human experience.

We publish one thoughtful article every Sunday, plus occasional mid-week essays. No noise. No clickbait. Just design thinking.

Our editorial lens: light, material, and the quiet poetry of daily life.

What Guides Us

Three principles behind every article

Depth over speed

We take time to research, interview, and reflect. Our articles are meant to be read slowly, not skimmed. We value nuance over hot takes.

Material honesty

We celebrate materials for what they are—not what they imitate. From board-formed concrete to reclaimed timber, we explore texture, weight, and craft.

Human-centered space

Architecture is ultimately about people. We ask how design affects comfort, ritual, community, and the quiet moments of everyday life.
